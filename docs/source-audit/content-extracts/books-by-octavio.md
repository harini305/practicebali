### GOOD LIZARD
EPISODE 1: THE YOGA SUTRA: AN OFFERING OF HOPE TP A PERCEPTUALLY ENSLAVED HUMANITY FROM THE SERPENT KING, PATANJALI

Good Lizard is an escape manual. Mediocrity, limitation, fear, doubt, worry and confusion are the prison bars that enslave our minds. Consciousness is the truth. Limitation is the lie. It is time to renounce the lie. Practice, and more specifically, Meditation is how we bend the bars and break free.
