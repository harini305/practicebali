## TERMS & CONDITIONS
- Payment must be finalized not later than 72 hours (3 Days) PRIOR to the scheduled Private Yoga session.
- No cancelations are permitted within 48 hours of the scheduled start time of Private yoga session and no refund will be issued.
- If cancelation is made between 72-48 hours prior to scheduled Private Yoga session, a 50% refund of payment will be given (minus any fees).
- If cancelation is made prior to 72 hours before scheduled Private Yoga session, then full refund will be given (minus any fees).

- No changes can be made within the 48 hour timeframe prior to Group Private Yoga session time.
