## INTRODUCTION TO KRIYA MEDITATION
2 DAY WORKSHOP WITH OCTAVIO SALVADO
31 OCTOBER – 1 NOVEMBER 830am-7pm
Rp 3,500,000
Do you find Meditation difficult?
Did you know that there’s an exact Meditation strategy for householders like us?
This ancient system, referred to as KRIYA Meditation, or TANTRIC Meditation acknowledges the fact that as householders, our minds do not slip into stillness and silence easily.
A mind that is constantly on the move, because it’s engaged in seemingly endless daily tasks, must be treated differently from the mind of someone who is living in a cave, in an ashram, or has no worldly responsibilities.
The renunciate path is not our path. Our path is the path of the householder. Our desire is to thrive in the world. To live with vitality and a tranquil mind. To have vibrant relationships and fulfilling, abundant professional careers, as well as a deep spiritual connection. That is our goal.
And all of this is possible, IF we have the right Meditation strategy.
How does Kriya Meditation work?
Simple. We catch the mind where it’s at – which is in activity – and from there, we guide it towards stillness.
Once the mind is made still, THEN we can transform it. We can heal it, illuminate it and empower it through the introduction of specific time-tested, inner Meditation objects and symbols that hold inherent spiritual power and wisdom.
For thousands of years, men and women, exactly like us, have been using Kriya Meditation techniques, not to escape life, but to empower themselves to excel within it.
Now these teachings have come to you. Take full advantage of this opportunity.
No one else is coming to save us. There are no magic pills or peptides that offer lasting mental transformation. If we want power, joy and freedom in our life, we are going to have to organize it for our self.
Kriya Meditation is the HOW.
A weekend FULL of powerhouse practice and highly educational lectures, all based on the traditional teachings of Meditation and Yoga science.
Students will receive video recordings of all practices covered in this workshop, to assist with ongoing personal practice after the workshop. Bring a notebook and pen, however, as we have a LOT to cover!
